Questions

Who is it for?
Indian marketing agencies with a few clients or a few dozen, who run the business on spreadsheets and WhatsApp today.
Does the AI send anything by itself?
No. It drafts and analyses. Messages, posts, invoices and budget changes wait for a person, and every action is logged.
Can one client see another's data?
No. Each agency has its own workspace, and each client's records stay with that client.
Does it post to Instagram for us?
Not yet. Posts are planned, approved and recorded; automatic publishing comes when the platform connections are approved.
When can we start?
We invite agencies a few at a time, starting with a call to set up your first clients.
What will it cost?
Pricing is set before launch, in rupees with GST shown. The waitlist hears it first.

Questions

Who is Adbibe OS for?

Marketing agencies in India with a few clients or a few dozen: performance, social, SEO, influencer and full-service teams who run everything on spreadsheets and WhatsApp today.

When can we start?

We are opening it to agencies a few at a time. Join the list and we will write to you when there is a place, with a call to set up your first clients.

Does the AI send things on its own?

No. AI researches, drafts and analyses. Messages, posts, invoices and budget changes wait for a person on your team to approve them, and every action is written to an audit log.

Can one client see another's data?

No. Every agency's workspace is separate, and inside it each client's records stay with that client. Staff see only the sections you give them.

What will it cost?

Pricing is set before launch, in rupees with GST shown. People on the waitlist hear it first.

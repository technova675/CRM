Questions

Who is it for? Indian marketing agencies with a few clients or a few dozen, running performance, social, SEO and influencer work on spreadsheets and WhatsApp today.

When can we start? We invite agencies a few at a time. Join the list and we will write when there is a place, with a call to set up your first clients.

Does the AI send anything by itself? No. It researches, drafts and analyses; messages, posts, invoices and budget changes wait for a person, and every action is logged.

Which AI does it use? Current open models through approved providers. Each result shows which model wrote it.

Can clients see each other's data? No. Each agency's workspace is separate, and each client's records stay with that client.

Do our clients get a login? Not at first. Their approvals are recorded by your team, with who approved and how.

Does it post to Instagram for us? Not yet. Posts are planned, approved and recorded; automatic publishing comes when the platform connections are approved.

Can we import what we have? Yes. Leads from CSV, results from Meta, Instagram and LinkedIn exports, and clients one by one or in bulk.

What will it cost? Pricing is set before launch, in rupees with GST shown. People on the list hear it first.

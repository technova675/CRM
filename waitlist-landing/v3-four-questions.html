    Questions

    Who is Adbibe OS for?

    Indian marketing agencies with a few clients or a few dozen: performance, social, SEO, influencer and full-service teams.

    Can the AI change a budget by itself?

    Not unless an administrator turns that on for that action. Budget changes start on Approval, and every action, by a person or the AI, is logged with who, what, when, before and after.

    Where do the numbers come from?

    From the platforms' own exports and connections. When a figure is the AI's estimate rather than data, it is labelled as an inference.

    Is our clients' data kept apart?

    Yes. Each agency has its own workspace, each client's records stay with that client, and staff see only the sections you give them.

    When can we start, and what will it cost?

    Agencies are invited a few at a time. Pricing is set before launch, in rupees with GST shown, and the waitlist hears it first.
